v0.3.0 — Security foundations.

- PIN lock (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512, 210k+ iterations).
- Biometric unlock (fingerprint) as opt-in add-on.
- Panic-decoy mode (distinct panic code) — attacker sees the app but no data.
- Exponential backoff after 5 failed attempts (5 s → 5 min).
- Auto-lock when app goes to background.
- Persistent emergency notification on the lock screen.
- Hold-3s EMERGENCY SMS button (anti-accidental trigger).
- "Erase all my data" action in settings (PanicService).
